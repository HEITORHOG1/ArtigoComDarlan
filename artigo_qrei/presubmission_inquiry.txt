Working title:
"A quantitative-coupled BowTie for design-stage reliability assessment of a
high-rpm valve train: the VW AP 1.8 cylinder head at 10,000 rpm"

The paper develops a quantitative-coupled BowTie procedure for design-stage
reliability assessment of high-rpm mechanical systems, in which each preventive
barrier of the BowTie is bound to a deterministic engineering indicator with a
literature-anchored pass/fail criterion. The method is demonstrated on a single
case — the valve train of a high-performance VW AP 1.8 cylinder head at
10,000 rpm — and is refined with a Monte Carlo tolerance analysis
(N = 2 x 10^5) under ASTM A877/A877M. It also contrasts the approach with the
conventional FMEA/RPN prioritisation and checks it for face validity against
four published valve-train failure investigations. The study is analytical;
experimental validation is identified as declared future work.

We believe the contribution is aligned with recent methodological work
published in the journal, such as the BOBTA bow-tie extension (Hellas &
Khabtane, 2026; doi:10.1002/qre.70069), the probabilistic uncertain-linguistic
FMEA approach (Tang, 2025; doi:10.1002/qre.3657), and the Monte-Carlo
structural-reliability analysis of Qian et al. (2023; doi:10.1002/qre.3293).

Could you kindly advise whether a manuscript of this nature would be considered
for the journal? I would be glad to send the full abstract or the draft on
request.
